# Contacts

**Where to find it:** Contact → **Contacts** (its own item in the sidebar, next to AI Inbox).

While the AI Inbox list shows every individual submission, Contacts groups them by sender — one row per person who has ever submitted one of your monitored forms, so you can see someone's overall history at a glance instead of hunting through separate messages.

## If you haven't received any submissions yet

You'll see a "No contacts yet" message with shortcuts to **Select Forms** (jumps to General Settings) and **View AI Inbox** — expected on a brand-new install.

## Reading the table

Each row shows: **Contact** (name, click to search that person's messages in the AI Inbox), **Email**, **Category** (their most recent submission's category), **Priority** (their most recent submission's priority badge), **Messages** (how many they've submitted in total), **Replied** (how many of those you've replied to), and **Last Contact** (how long ago their most recent submission came in).

Clicking a contact's name or email takes you to the AI Inbox, pre-filtered to that person's email address.

## Searching and filtering

Above the table: a **search box**, a **Category** filter, and a **Priority** filter, plus a **Filter** button to apply them and a **Clear filters** link once any are active.

## Deleting a contact

The **More actions** (three dots) menu on a row, or the **Bulk actions → Delete** option after selecting several with the checkboxes, doesn't erase anyone's data — it archives every message from that sender, the same as archiving them one by one from the AI Inbox. Nothing is permanently deleted by this action.

## Exporting

Click the **Export** button in the page header to download the currently filtered list as a CSV file.
