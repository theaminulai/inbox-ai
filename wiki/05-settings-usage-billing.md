# 5. Usage & Billing

**Where to find it:** Contact → Settings → **Usage & Billing** tab.

This is a read-only dashboard — there's nothing to configure here, just numbers to keep an eye on so you know how much you're using your AI provider (and roughly what it's costing you).

## Choosing a time period

In the top-right corner of the page is a dropdown you can use to change which time range the whole page reflects:

* Last 7 days
* Last 30 days *(shown by default when you open the tab)*
* Last 90 days
* This month
* Last 1, 2, 3, or 5 years

Pick any option and the four summary cards and the breakdown below update automatically — no page reload needed.

## The four summary cards

* **Total requests** — how many times the AI was called (this covers both analyzing submissions and drafting replies) during the selected period.
* **Tokens used** — the total amount of text processed by the AI, shown with a breakdown of how much was sent in versus generated out.
* **Estimated cost** — an approximate dollar cost for that usage, across whichever provider(s) you've used.
* **Monthly budget** — shows "Not configured yet" until a spending-limit feature is added in a future update.

## Cost by Request Type

Below the summary cards, a set of bars breaks down your estimated cost by what the request was *for* (for example, analyzing a submission versus drafting a reply). Each bar shows its share of the total as both a percentage-width bar and a dollar amount.

If you haven't had any AI activity yet, this section will simply say there's no usage recorded yet — that's expected on a brand-new install.
