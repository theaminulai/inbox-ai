# 8. The AI Inbox List

**Where to find it:** Contact → **AI Inbox**.

This is the main screen — every submission from the forms you're monitoring shows up here, sorted by priority and AI confidence, most relevant first.

## If you haven't received any submissions yet

You'll see a "No submissions yet" message with two shortcuts: **Select Forms** (jumps to General Settings to turn on a form) and **Configure AI Provider** (jumps to the AI Provider tab). This is expected on a brand-new install — nothing is wrong.

## Searching and filtering

Above the message table is a row of controls:

* **Search box** — searches across customer names, emails, and message text.
* **Form filter** — show only submissions from one specific form.
* **Status filter** — All, New, Needs Review, Reviewed, Drafted, Replied, Failed, or Archived.
* **Priority filter** — All, Urgent, High, Normal, or Low.
* **Category filter** — filter by whatever AI-assigned category you're looking for.
* **Filter button** — applies whatever you've selected above.
* **Clear filters** — appears once any filter is active, and resets everything back to showing all messages.

In the top-right corner of the page header is a separate **date range** dropdown (All time, Last 7/30/90 days, This month, or Last 1–5 years) that limits the list to messages received in that window — independent from the filters above it.

Every filter and the date range is reflected in the page's URL, so you can bookmark or share a specific filtered view.

## Reading the table

Each row shows: **Customer** (name and email, click either to open the submission), **Message** (a short preview), **Form**, **Priority** (a colored badge), **Category**, **AI Confidence** (a percentage), **Status** (a colored badge), and **Received** (how long ago it came in).

## Row actions

At the end of each row are three icons:

* **View** (eye icon) — opens the full submission.
* **Reply** (envelope icon) — opens the submission and jumps straight to the Reply Composer.
* **More** (three dots) — opens a small menu with actions that depend on the message's current status:
  * **Retry** — only shown on Failed messages; re-runs the AI analysis.
  * **Mark reviewed** — shown on New or Needs Review messages.
  * **Archive** — shown on anything that isn't already archived.

## Exporting

Click the **Export** button in the page header to download the currently filtered list as a CSV file.

## Paging through results

At the bottom of the table, "Showing X to Y of Z messages" tells you where you are, with page-number links to move through the rest. If a search or filter turns up nothing, you'll see "No messages match your filters" along with a one-click **Clear filters** link.
