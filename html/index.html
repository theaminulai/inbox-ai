What it does

A real support inbox, built on top of Contact Form 7

Instead of scattered email notifications, every submission lands in one searchable, AI-triaged queue.

Unified AI Inbox

Every monitored form's submissions land in one searchable inbox instead of getting buried across email notifications.

AI summaries & scoring

Each submission gets a plain-language summary, a suggested category and priority (Urgent/High/Normal/Low), and a confidence score.

Ready-to-edit draft replies

Inbox AI drafts a reply for high-confidence messages. A person always reviews and clicks Send — nothing goes out on its own.

Inbound email replies

When a customer replies to your emailed response, Inbox AI picks it up automatically, threads it into the conversation, and drafts a follow-up.

Customer mood tracking

Every message in a conversation is read for tone — positive, neutral, frustrated, or angry — so an unhappy customer never slips by.

Search, filter & export

Filter by form, status, priority, category, and date range, with full-text search — and one-click CSV export of the current view.

Contacts view

Every sender is automatically grouped into one Contacts row — a lightweight CRM view built directly from your existing submissions.

Email & Slack alerts

Get notified on urgent submissions, provider outages, drafts ready for review, or a daily summary digest — email or Slack, independently configurable.

Flamingo migration

Already using Flamingo to store submissions? Import that history into Inbox AI as copies — nothing in Flamingo is ever changed.

How it works

From form submission to a sent reply

Visitor submits

A visitor fills in one of your monitored Contact Form 7 forms, same as always.

Captured locally

Inbox AI stores the submission in your own WordPress database.

AI analyzes it

Your connected provider returns a summary, category, priority, confidence, mood, and a suggested reply.

You review

Everything lands in the AI Inbox — you review and edit before anything goes out.

Reply & thread

Send when ready. A customer's reply is picked up automatically and re-analyzed.
