Chapter 1

Getting Started

What you need before you start

  • Contact Form 7 installed and active. Inbox AI works alongside it and never modifies your existing forms.
  • WordPress 6.7 or newer.
  • A hosting account running PHP 8.1 or newer.
  • An account with at least one AI provider: OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google (Gemini) — you'll need an API key.

Installing and activating

  1. Upload the Inbox AI plugin the same way you would any other WordPress plugin (Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin, or by placing the folder in wp-content/plugins/).
  2. Go to Plugins and click Activate next to Inbox AI.
  3. Look for Contact in your admin sidebar — you should see two new entries under it: AI Inbox and Settings.
If you don't see these two entries, double-check that Contact Form 7 itself is active — Inbox AI won't add its menu items without it.

First-time setup checklist

  1. Connect an AI provider. Contact → Settings → AI Provider tab. Pick a provider, paste your API key, click Test Connection.
  2. Choose which forms to monitor. Settings → General tab. Nothing is captured until a form is turned on here.
  3. (Optional) Review the AI's instructions. The Prompts tab — defaults work well for most sites.
  4. (Optional) Turn on notifications. The Notifications tab can email you or post to Slack when something urgent comes in.
  5. Submit a test message. Then check Contact → AI Inbox — your submission should appear within moments, followed by an AI summary shortly after.
Chapter 2

AI Provider Settings

Where to find it: Contact → Settings → AI Provider tab

This is where you tell Inbox AI which AI service to use for analyzing submissions and drafting replies — nothing works until a provider is connected.

Connecting a provider

  1. Under Choose a provider, click the card for the service you have an account with: OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google.
  2. Paste your API key into the Configuration section. It's encrypted at rest and never shown in full again (sk-••••7f2A).
  3. Choose a Model from the dropdown.
  4. Choose a Request timeout — how many seconds to wait for a response (30, 60, or 90 seconds).
  5. Click Test Connection, then Save Changes once it succeeds.

A green Connected badge appears next to the Configuration heading once a working key is saved. You can switch providers at any time — the Model dropdown refreshes to match.

Fallback Behavior

  • Retry failed requests automatically — retries a failed AI request up to 3 times, with a longer wait between each attempt, before giving up.
  • Fall back to manual review on repeated failure — if every retry still fails, the submission is marked Needs Review instead of Failed, so it doesn't get lost.
  • Send email alert on provider outage — emails site admins if the AI provider becomes unreachable.
Chapter 3

General Settings

Where to find it: Contact → Settings → General tab

Controls which forms feed into the AI Inbox and how new submissions are automatically handled.

Monitored Forms

Every Contact Form 7 form on your site is listed with a switch next to it. Turn a switch on to start capturing that form's submissions; turning it off stops new ones without removing existing history. A line under each form shows how many submissions it's received this calendar month.

Automatic Processing

  • Analyze new submissions automatically — the AI immediately summarizes, categorizes, and scores every new submission.
  • Auto-draft replies for high-confidence messages — the AI also writes a suggested reply. A draft is never sent on its own.
  • Auto-archive detected spam — messages the AI is at least 95% sure are spam are archived automatically.

A Confidence threshold for "Needs Review" slider (0–100%) flags anything analyzed below that confidence for manual double-checking.

Data Retention

Keep submissions for — Forever, 24 months, 12 months, or 6 months. Submissions older than the chosen window are permanently deleted by a daily background check; "Forever" never deletes anything.

Chapter 4

Prompts

Where to find it: Contact → Settings → Prompts tab

"Prompts" are the written instructions given to the AI. The defaults work well out of the box — most people never need to touch this tab.

Analysis Prompt

The instruction the AI follows when reading a new submission. Supports placeholders: {message}, {customer_name}, {form_name}, {submitted_fields}, {categories}. Changes only affect new submissions from that point on.

Reply Draft Prompt

The instruction used when writing a suggested reply. Supports {message}, {summary}, {tone}, {signature}.

Reply Tone

Choose a default tone for AI-drafted replies: Friendly and professional, Formal, Casual, or Concise.

Click Save Prompts to keep changes, or Reset to Defaults to discard your edits and start clean again.

Chapter 5

Usage & Billing

Where to find it: Contact → Settings → Usage & Billing tab

A read-only dashboard — nothing to configure, just numbers to keep an eye on. A dropdown in the top-right corner switches the time period: last 7/30/90 days, this month, or the last 1–5 years.

The four summary cards

  • Total requests — how many times the AI was called during the selected period.
  • Tokens used — total text processed, broken down by sent vs. generated.
  • Estimated cost — an approximate dollar figure across whichever provider(s) you've used.
  • Monthly budget — shows "Not configured yet" until a spending-limit feature ships.

Cost by Request Type breaks estimated cost down by what the request was for — analyzing a submission versus drafting a reply.

Chapter 6

Notifications

Where to find it: Contact → Settings → Notifications tab

Decide how you or your team hear about activity, instead of checking the inbox constantly.

Email Notifications

  • Notify me on urgent messages — the moment a submission is scored Urgent.
  • Daily summary digest — one email each morning at 9:00 AM summarizing recent activity.
  • Notify on AI analysis failure — whenever a submission's analysis couldn't complete.
  • Notify when a reply draft is ready — when a new AI-drafted reply is waiting for approval.

These four switches work independently of each other.

Slack Integration

  1. Turn on Send a Slack message for urgent submissions.
  2. Paste a valid https:// webhook URL from your Slack workspace's Incoming Webhooks integration.
  3. Save your settings.

Click Save Notification Settings at the bottom of the page — nothing takes effect until you save.

Chapter 7

Import & Migration (Flamingo)

Where to find it: Contact → Settings → Import & Migration tab

If you previously used Flamingo to store Contact Form 7 submissions, this wizard brings that history into Inbox AI as copies — nothing in Flamingo is ever changed or deleted, and re-running the import never creates duplicates.

  1. Choose a Source — live Flamingo data (if Flamingo is active) or a CSV exported from Flamingo's own Export button.
  2. Options — optionally run AI analysis on imported messages right away, or leave it for later in smaller batches.
  3. Review & Import — confirm the count, then start the import; a progress bar tracks it.
  4. Complete — import another batch, or jump straight to the AI Inbox.
Chapter 8

The AI Inbox List

Where to find it: Contact → AI Inbox

The main screen — every monitored submission shows up here, sorted by priority and AI confidence, most relevant first.

Searching and filtering

Search box (name/email/message text), plus filters for Form, Status (All, New, Needs Review, Reviewed, Drafted, Replied, Failed, Archived), Priority, and Category. A separate date-range dropdown in the header limits the list to a time window. Every filter is reflected in the URL, so a view can be bookmarked or shared.

Row actions

  • View — opens the full submission.
  • Reply — opens the submission at the Reply Composer.
  • More — Retry (Failed only), Mark reviewed (New/Needs Review), Archive.

Click Export to download the currently filtered list as a CSV file.

Chapter 9

Viewing & Replying to a Submission

Where to find it: click any customer's name/email, or the eye/envelope icon, from the AI Inbox list

AI Analysis

Summary, Category and Priority, Confidence (with a bar), AI Reasoning, and a Regenerate Analysis link. If analysis failed instead, you'll see Retry, Mark Reviewed, and a Provider Settings shortcut.

Reply Composer

Appears once analysis has completed. The recipient is always the real sender and can't be edited. Edit the pre-filled subject/body as needed, use the small formatting toolbar, or click the refresh icon to have the AI regenerate the draft. Save Draft keeps edits without sending; Send Reply shows a confirmation step before anything actually goes out.

Activity

A timeline at the bottom logs everything that's happened — received, analyzed, drafted, sent — each with a relative timestamp.

Chapter 10

Troubleshooting

"AI analysis could not be completed" on a submission

  1. Open the submission and click Retry — many failures are temporary.
  2. If it keeps failing, check the AI Provider tab for a green Connected badge; re-check your API key if not.
  3. If you just changed your key or provider, make sure you clicked Save Changes — testing alone doesn't save it.
  4. As a fallback, click Mark Reviewed and handle the reply manually while you sort out the provider issue.

No submissions are showing up

  1. Confirm the form is toggled on under Settings → General → Monitored Forms.
  2. Check the AI Inbox's date-range dropdown — switch it to All time.
  3. Clear any active search/filter.

Test Connection fails

  • Double-check the API key was copied in full, with no extra spaces.
  • Confirm the key hasn't been revoked or expired.
  • Make sure you're testing the provider card you meant to configure.

Slack notifications aren't arriving

  • Confirm the Slack switch is on in Settings → Notifications.
  • Confirm the webhook URL is a real https:// Slack webhook.
  • Remember this only fires for Urgent-priority submissions.
Still stuck? Make sure both Contact Form 7 and Inbox AI are updated to their latest versions — fixes ship regularly.
